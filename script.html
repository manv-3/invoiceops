🎬 1-Minute Demo: The Telegram Workflow

This script highlights the seamless 'text-to-invoice' workflow using Telegram and Notion, demonstrating the power of AI without showing any code or terminals.

[0:00 - 0:10] The Hook (The Problem)

🎥 Visual: Split screen. On the left, your empty Notion "Invoices" database. On the right, your Telegram app open to your bot.
🎙️ Voiceover: "Processing invoices usually means manual data entry and messy spreadsheets. What if you could just text your invoices to an AI?"

[0:10 - 0:25] The Trigger (Telegram)

🎥 Visual: On the Telegram side of the screen, drag and drop the invoice PDF file into the chat and hit send.
🎙️ Voiceover: "We built an InvoiceOps bot. When a vendor emails you a PDF, you just forward it directly to the bot. No app to install, no complicated uploads."

[0:25 - 0:45] The Magic (Notion & AI)

🎥 Visual: Focus on the Notion window. Wait for the new invoice to magically appear in the database! Click the row to open the page.
🎙️ Voiceover: "Within seconds, Google's Gemini AI reads the document, extracts the data, and pushes it directly to our operations team in Notion. Look closely—it perfectly extracted the Vendor, calculated the Tax and Subtotal, and even flagged the invoice for human review."

[0:45 - 1:00] The Human Loop

🎥 Visual: Scroll down to show the attached PDF, then change the Human Decision dropdown from "Pending" to "Approve".
🎙️ Voiceover: "If the math checks out, a human just clicks 'Approve'. The system instantly detects the approval and routes it for payment. No data entry, no code. Just AI doing the heavy lifting."

💡 Pro-Tips for Recording

  • Keep the terminal hidden! The best part about this demo is that it proves non-technical people can use it without ever touching code.
  • The Split Screen: If you can record a split screen showing Telegram and Notion side-by-side, the moment the row magically pops up in Notion while you are looking at Telegram is the ultimate "wow" factor.
  • Use the sample data: Use the invoice-fixture.pdf in the sample folder for the best extraction results during the demo.
